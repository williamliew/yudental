Dental lab

Crown, bridge & implant work for referring practices.

Welcome to the dental lab!

We are a specialist laboratory delivering crown & bridge and implant work for practices across New Zealand, with the same care for a single unit as for a full-arch case.

Based in Wellington with nationwide courier, Yu Dental Laboratory works closely with referring clinicians on planning, materials and shade. We enjoy being involved from early case discussion through to delivery, so the result matches what you and your patient expect.

Alongside conventional crown and bridge, we support removable and fixed implant work where your plan calls for it, including overdentures and multi-unit solutions, using materials certified to NZ and European standards.

Additionally we are digital-ready and can accept digital scans direct from your practice.

Our promise

  • We only use top-quality products and materials from reputable manufacturers.
  • 100% New Zealand owned and operated; appliances made on site.
  • We stand behind our workmanship and are upfront about timelines, materials and what each case requires.
